Combo house (practice, on your server)

Your AWS server listens to people asking for parlay prices. For each one it writes down the price we would have offered (the legs multiplied, plus 5%). When the parlay really sells, we see what the pro got. If our price was lower, we count it as our sale. When the games end, we see if we'd have kept the money. No real money moves.

Hidden costs

Copying late: when we copy a wallet, we always buy after it did. By then the price has often gone up, so we pay a bit more than they did. That extra comes straight out of our profit. Red means it cost us money.

Bigger bets: our practice bets are tiny (5 shares). A real $25 or $100 bet has to buy from more sellers at worse prices, so it earns less. This table shows what each bet size would have made for every $100 bet.

Cost of being late, and of betting bigger

Copying late: the copy tracks buy after the wallet did, usually at a worse price. This is how much more we paid than they did, in total. Bigger bets: every trade is also priced at real stakes on the same order book. "Paper" is our 5-share bet. A $25 or $100 bet goes deeper into the book, so the profit per $100 shrinks, and sometimes only part of a $100 order fills.

Buys NO on the stricter + YES on the looser when both cost under $1 after fees: pays at least $1, or $2 if only the looser one comes true. Same site and rules, so no cross-site mismatch. $100 paper

**Proof status:** Not proven yet: no finished sets. Each one earns about 2.0c a share, so it needs about 114 finished sets with no short payouts before we can be 90% sure it's not luck.

No open pairs.

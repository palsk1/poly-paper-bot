## How to talk to Jan
- Use plain dollars and cents and simple words. **No percentages.** Math is hard for him, so show "cost $4.85, pays $5.00" instead of a rate. Light snark and humor are welcome.
- Ask clarifying questions before starting a bigger task instead of guessing.
- He reads on his phone, so keep reports short.
- **Plain words (2026-11-09, Jan's ask):** he found the dashboard and reports too jargon-heavy ("like a tech bro AI hybrid"). Write for a smart friend who doesn't trade: short sentences, everyday words, no edge / trust score / fill / book / "careful guess". Jan likes "P&L" and "worst case per $100": keep those. Say what each number means for him (good, bad, too early). Costs are red, never green. Strategy verdicts use `bot.plain_verdict()`: "Looks real", "Promising, needs more bets", "Ahead, but could be luck", "Behind, but could be bad luck" (added 2026-11-12: losing, but the lucky end of the plain 90% range is still above $0), "Losing", "Too early to tell". "Worst case per $100" is a proof bar on finished bets (one made-up total loss, bar raised for ~130 tracks), not "every open bet loses"; -$100 is its floor and usually means too few or too swingy bets. The dashboard, the daily ntfy push and the morning review prompt all use them.

## How it runs
- **GitHub Actions** (public repo, so it's free): `watch.yml` runs every 5 min, `daily.yml` once a day. Both write `state/` back to `main` and share a concurrency group, `bot-state`. The save step retries the push up to 4 times, because GitHub sometimes rejects a push with a server error.
- **The 5-minute timer is cron-job.org**, set up by Jan under his own account. Job "poly bot watch": POST to `.../actions/workflows/watch.yml/dispatches`, body `{"ref":"main"}`. Its auth is a fine-grained GitHub token, Actions read/write on this repo only, **expiring 2027-11-03**, and it emails Jan on failure. GitHub's own cron in `watch.yml` is only a backup, since GitHub's scheduler skips many runs. One-off 500/502 failure emails are normal blips.
- **ntfy pushes** go to Jan's phone. Since 2026-11-08 (Jan's ask, not to be overwhelmed): the daily push is a **top 10 by trust** (see README "The lab"), and only those 10 strategies send trade pushes (`state/top.json`, re-ranked hourly by watch since 2026-11-09). FLIP/SHORT/MISMATCH alerts always go out. Since 2026-11-13 every push is logged in `state/push_log.json` (sent / failed / muted per day, last 200 sent or failed) and summary.md has a "Phone alerts" section; `selftest.yml` sends a test push. The Kalshi maker tracks (`kc_maker*`) never send trade pushes, so a top 10 with many of them means a quieter phone. Every strategy is still tracked and ranked in `summary.md`.
- **Dashboard (2026-11-08):** https://palsk1.github.io/poly-paper-bot/ , a phone page rebuilt by `pages.yml` after every watch/daily run (`dashboard.py` fills `dashboard.html`). Read-only on `state/`. README "How it runs". Since 2026-11-11 (Jan's ask): every strategy shows its worst case per $100 once a bet has finished (faded, "mostly luck" under 10 bets), and copy-wallet cards show `wallet_notes.json` (what each wallet does, from `probe wallets_all`; refresh it when wallets change) plus an automatic "what we copied so far" line. Since 2026-11-12 (Jan: "the dashboard doesn't feel standardized"): the combo house and the 4 main fast-Kalshi tests are rows like any strategy (`combo_house`, `kfast_*`, built in `dashboard.py` `ext_row` from `state/quoter.json` and `state/kfast_live/days`), with the same card, verdict, worst case and graph. Display pots: combo house $300 at $5 a parlay (Jan's ask; the server still counts $25, per-$100 numbers and PROTOCOL 5c unchanged), fast Kalshi $100. New kinds of test should go through `ext_row`, not a one-off card. Since 2026-11-14 (Jan: "I can barely explain the explanations to my friend"): every strategy has a plain card from `plain_words.json` (one or two sentences, a tiny example, wins when / loses when, a score line), the old rule text is folded under "The exact rule", and the top has a 30-second "how these bets work" box. **Every new track needs a `plain_words.json` entry in the same PR.** Since 2026-11-22 (Jan: "show the live bots... all vital information"): a gold "Real money" section at the top (`dashboard.live_rows`) reads the real bots' hourly uploads (release `live-data`): practice/real/stopped, bet size, P&L, cents per $1, today, open bets, last bets, and the paper twin over the same time. - **Morning review:** a scheduled task named "Poly paper review" runs at 08:14 Stockholm in the cloud (it doesn't need Jan's PC). It reads the state, health-checks runs, reviews proposed arb pairs, writes Jan a short report and may make allowed fixes. ## Decisions and findings so far
- **Hosting:** the paper bot stays on GitHub + cron-job.org. The one exception is Jan's AWS Lightsail server in Stockholm (11 Nov), because Polymarket only sends combo requests to logged-in quoters; it is also where the planned $25 real bot would run, so the key lives somewhere private (see "Running right now"). Speed tests (`kspeed.py`) say no server is needed for anything else. Racing co-located high-frequency firms isn't winnable or needed: our edges last minutes to hours. **Before any real money:** check that Jan may legally trade on each site from Sweden (Kalshi especially).
- **Endgame (2026-11-05, Jan approved):** the day after the US midterms, "past deadline" election markets were still being counted. "WI-03 by 6–9%" fell from 97c to 62c. So election markets now only count in the resolved/proposed tiers (`weak_tier_skip_words`). The 20 open bets are held as they are. The budget fills fast now that the bot runs every 5 min.
- **Stopped tracks (2026-11-12, Jan: "Dump and fix bug", `backtest/review_worst_poly.md`):** `fade_sure`, `wx_forecast`, `endgame_ending_soon`, `endgame_97`, `ladder` and `every_horse` have `"stopped"` in config: no new bets, open bets settle, history stays on the scoreboard. Same day, bug fix: the bot read a market's date-only `endDateIso` before the full `endDate`, so markets looked like they ended at 00:00 UTC (133 of 161 lab "past the deadline" buys were made on the end day itself). It now reads `endDate` first.
- **Backtest (2026-11-08, `backtest.py`, PR #7):** replayed the rules on 125,712 finished Polymarket markets (Mar–Nov 2026, ~30,000 trades, 1c slippage + fees). Only `panic_fade_10c` came out ahead (+$3 per $100, not proven). Every favorite/endgame rule lost, even at 0c slippage. **Endgame "ending_soon" was dropped from the main `endgame` track (Jan approved):** it lost about $5-6 per $100, mostly weather, crypto-price and tweet-count buckets bought at 95c a day before the end (checked: real flips, not a data bug). **"past_deadline" was dropped too (Jan picked it, same day):** it lost $167 on 709 bets, so the main track now only buys once a result is resolved or proposed; the 20 open past-deadline bets are held to the end. Lab variants keep both (frozen). **Don't re-test this unless something new comes up** (e.g. real order-book history, or a filter that removes those bucket markets). Results: `backtest/report.md`.
- **Every horse:** it scans about 15,000 events every 2 hours. 3–5 sets a scan look cheap on the listed price, but **none is cheap on the real order book for 5 shares plus fees**. Only one ever passed 1c (Brazil central bank, January), and it failed the 15%-a-year bar. Conclusion so far: this trick doesn't pay for a slow bot. **Open question for Jan:** keep it as is, scan every 30 min, or repurpose the $100. He hasn't decided.
- **Arb:** the first real paper arb was Satoshi (Kalshi NO 94c + Polymarket Yes 2.9c, edge 2.5c). The pair review rejects most proposals: different deadlines, sources, "announced" vs "happened", and Kalshi rule text missing definitions.
- **Fast markets (2026-11-08, Jan asked for them):** weather temperature buckets and hourly crypto candles pay out within hours or 2 days. Two lab tracks, `wx_forecast` (stopped 2026-11-12, it lost $11 per $100 on 114 bets) and `crypto_late` (README "Fast markets"). Weather now settles on **METAR reports from aviationweather.gov** (not Wunderground), and the rules link the exact query. aviationweather.gov cuts long answers short, so ask a few stations at a time. Open-Meteo and `data-api.binance.vision` work from GitHub Actions. ESPN returns 403 there. The same crypto model 3-30h out found nothing (the ladders are priced like the model), and already-decided weather buckets sit at 99.95c.
- **Dropped ideas (2026-11-08, `edges.py`):** negRisk conversion (sell a full set now for >$1) never beats fees on real books; holding rewards are real but tiny ($3.25/yr per $100, 255 long-dated markets).
- **Data audit (2026-11-09, after the endgame phantom fills):** `probe audit` checks past trades against Polymarket (buy prices vs the trade tape, copy timing vs the wallet's own trades, payouts vs final prices, stuck positions, marks vs bids, arb depth). Clean: cash/realized add up on every track, 40/40 payouts match, copies land 0-5 min after the wallet at about its price, nothing stuck. Fixed: no more "estimate" fills anywhere (buys or sells need a real book), copy tracks skip wallets holding both sides of a market (splits; some wb_/consensus tracks bought both legs before), arb repeat lots within an hour pay for the next shares on the book, open bets are marked at the best bid. `integrity()` runs after every watch/daily, writes `state/integrity.json`, a "Data check" section in summary.md, and pushes once per new problem. Maker rewards are a model and look high (~$24/day per $100): treat maker as unverified. **Maker pools (2026-11-09):** pool sizes change without notice (three of four were cut that day, e.g. $80 to $7 a day); the bot now re-reads each pool hourly from CLOB `/rewards/markets/{cid}`. YES and NO books really are one mirrored book (`probe rewardcheck`). Forum and research reports put real reward income at roughly $0.50-3 a day per $100, usually eaten by pick-off losses.
- **Fast Kalshi bot? (2026-11-09, `kfast.py`, `backtest/kfast.md`, Jan asked about a server):** 14 days second by second. Pulling bids fast when Binance turns adds nothing. Buying the sure side with 45 s left (z 4+) at 99.1-99.3c made about +$0.40 per $100, ~20-55 fills a day; resting 99.5/99.8c bids won 0.2-0.5c a contract.

# Kalshi's new 15-minute "up in the next 15 minutes?" bets outside crypto (27 Nov 2026)

`k15x.py` (paper only, public data). Question: do our crypto 15-minute rules work on Kalshi's new families: FX,
Treasury yields, oil, metals, the S&P 500?

## What we tested

- **18 series, 57,000+ windows**, 1 Oct (or each one's start) to 27 Nov 2026: Brent, WTI, natural gas, gold, silver,
  copper, platinum, palladium, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CAD, AUD/USD, S&P 500, 2 / 5 / 10 / 30-year yields.
  The scan found the siblings: gold (1.66 billion contracts in 58 days, more than any crypto window), WTI (430
  million), silver (244 million), natural gas (107 million), copper, platinum, palladium, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY.
  Hourly versions exist but nobody trades them (under 1,000 contracts in 3 days, gold hourly 162,000).
- For each window: the 1-minute bid and ask, every real trade of the last 6 minutes, and Kalshi's own settle number
  by the second (`/live_data`, the Pyth price these settle on). That number gives every result back.
- Fees: all these series charge takers only (the 0.07 formula), so a resting bid pays nothing. Prices move in 1c steps
  (crypto 15-minute windows have 0.1c steps), so a safe side looks like 99c bid / $1.00 ask.
- Fills: only contracts really sold into our bid after we placed it (everything sold below our price, plus sales at
  our price when we were alone there). First half of each series' days to pick, second half to check.

## Verdicts (exam, $4 bids)

| Rule | Bets | First half per $100 | Fresh half per $100 | Exam |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **kc_maker_safe_usd** (3 min left, best bid 95c+, bid 1c above it, cushion 2.3 on Kalshi's own number) | 5,402 | +$0.68 | +$0.51 | **Looks real** (fresh half alone, rule fixed before: Looks real too) |
| kc_maker (same, no cushion) | 11,130 | +$0.06 | -$0.17 | Behind, but could be bad luck. Dropped |
| 45-second buy, plain (first real buy of the leader at 90-99c, fee paid, $10) | 16,794 | -$0.35 | -$0.28 | Losing. Dropped |
| 45-second buy, cushion 4+ only | 626 | +$0.79 | +$1.44 | Promising, needs more bets (tiny: under $1 a day) |
| Backward look: buy the leader at 50-90c with 1-2 min left | 2,100-5,200 each | mixed | all behind | Losing / behind. Dropped |

kc_maker_safe_usd made **+$125 on $21,028 of $4 bids over 57 days, about $2.17 a day**. Every one of the 9 weeks was
ahead. Without its 20 best wins it is still +$121, so no single lucky bet carries it (a win pays about 4c, a loss costs
about $3.98). Harder checks: counting only contracts sold *below* our price, +$0.49 per $100; placing the bid 20
seconds late, +$0.43. Both halves stay ahead in both.

By group (same rule): **FX +$1.01 per $100 on 1,973 bets, Looks real**; metals +$0.53 (Promising); oil and gas
+$0.24 (could be luck, natural gas alone -$0.68); yields -$0.60 on 248 bets (behind; too small anyway);
S&P 500 23 bets (too early).

The cushion is what does it, like on crypto: leaders at 95c+ with a cushion of 3+ lost 13 times in 6,539 (the price
expected 82), with a cushion of 1-2 they lost 164 in 5,907 (price expected 189). Without the cushion the bid loses.

- Most it could make on an average day: about **$20 at $50 bids, $30 at $100 bids**.
- Money needed: about **$300-500** at $50 bids (bets pay back every 15 minutes, so the money turns over fast).
- Where it stops growing: **about $50-100 a bid**. Above that, sellers run out (only 3 bids in 4 fill whole at $100)
  and the profit per $100 falls from 60c to 38c; at $250 it makes less than at $100. Big fills come when we are wrong.
- A bad day: about **-$64 at $50 bids**, -$144 at $100.

## Traps

- **Ties go to Yes.** The settle number is rounded (2 decimals for oil, 5 for FX) and the rule is "at least the target".
  An unchanged price pays Yes: 4 of the 49 losses were No bets where the price came back exactly to the target.
- Kalshi pays a bid only from the right part of the account: Brent sits in "exchange index" 2 (the crypto part the
  real bot uses), USD/CAD in index 0. Check each series before any real bot.
- The cushion here is on Kalshi's own number, not an outside price, and with no 1.5 fix (these settle on one price at
  the close, not a 60-second average). Free minute data for FX / yields / metals was not needed.
- Gold's trade lists are huge (11.6 million trades in 58 days, 68 MB); one probe run fetches about 4 series.
- Treasury yields barely trade (1,000-2,000 contracts a window) and only on weekdays in New York hours.

## Verdict per series

- **FX (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CAD, AUD/USD): lead.** Best group.
- **Metals (gold, silver, copper, platinum, palladium): lead.** Gold and silver have by far the most sellers.
- Oil and gas: too early (WTI and Brent ahead, natural gas behind).
- Yields: dropped (small and behind). S&P 500: too early (23 bets).
- Plain kc_maker, the plain 45-second buy and the backward-look buys: dropped.

**Recommendation:** a paper twin of kc_maker_safe_usd on these series (FX and metals first, $4-10 bids, cushion 2.3 on
Kalshi's own number) for 1-2 weeks before any real money. It is history only: found with a rule copied from crypto,
but the settings grid had 160 tries.

Code: `k15x.py scan | fetch <series,...> | run [log]`, probes `nfx_k15x_*`, data `bt_cache/nf/k15x/` in the price store.

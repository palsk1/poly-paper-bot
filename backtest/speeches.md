## What it means

Before a week starts, Polymarket prices a word Trump says almost every week like a coin flip. Buying Yes there paid in 5 of 6 quarters, in both halves, with and without fees, and still at 6c worse prices. Buying every Yes blindly made $0, so the transcript record is what does the work. Weak spots: found by looking (about 50 versions, no untouched test set), 150 bets only, thin markets, one word (Transgender) is 18 of the bets, and the same idea during the week was good early and bad late. A live paper track would be the real test.

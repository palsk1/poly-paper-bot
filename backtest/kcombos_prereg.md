# Kalshi combos: one rule written down before the fresh days (22 Nov 2026, 16:30 UTC)

The first sample (6-17 Nov, `kcombos.py`) was read from the house's chair. The backward look (start from who won)
found one thing on the buyers' side:

**Buyers of nearly-sure combos won.** Trades where the asker bought a combo at 65c or more (821 trades): after the
buyer's fee they made +$13.80 per $100 spent, exam "Looks real" at the first look. Weak spots, known before the test:
big tickets carry it (tickets over $50: +$14.21; tickets up to $50: +$5.44, "Ahead, but could be luck"), the same
games show up in many of our sampled moments, it was found by looking (about 30 cuts), and these are the combos
buyers chose themselves, not ones a blind bot would pick.

## The test, fixed now

Fresh days: 23 Oct to 5 Nov 2026 (14 days, 12 random moments a day, the same download and the same cut-off rule).
Nothing in the tool's scoring changes.

1. **Main:** every trade where the asker bought the combo at 65c or more. Buyer pays price + the 7% fee formula,
   gets $1 a contract if the combo wins. Pass = ahead, and the low end of the exam's luck range above $0.
2. **Small tickets (what we could do):** the same, tickets of $50 or less, every trade counted the same
   (a $2 ticket and a $50 ticket weigh the same). Pass = ahead with the low end above $0.
3. **House side:** all "asker bought Yes" trades from the house's chair after its fee. First sample: -$1.64 per $100
   of tickets. Nothing to pass; reported as it comes.

If 1 and 2 both pass, it becomes a lead to show Jan (no track without his go). If only 1 passes, it is "the big
bettors are sharp", which we cannot copy. If neither, dropped.
